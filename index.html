About us

SlopeSwap started with a simple idea: good ski and snowboard gear should get a second season instead of sitting unused.

Our planned service will bring sellers and buyers together, add clear condition checks, and coordinate shipping and payment. This prototype demonstrates the concept; inspections, shipping and payment are not live services.
